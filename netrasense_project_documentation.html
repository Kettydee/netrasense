🌿 Ambient Healthcare Philosophy: Who, Why & How

👥 Who is it for?

Visually impaired individuals, legally blind persons, and elderly people coping with macular degeneration, glaucoma, or diabetic cataracts. It also serves as an indispensable safety monitor for family caregivers, mobility therapists, and emergency clinicians.

🎯 Why was it created?

Traditional white canes only detect hazards when physical impact occurs. Cloud-only apps create dangerous 3-second delays and suffer frequent rate limit lockouts. NetraSense delivers zero-latency on-device spatial intelligence with a calming, empathetic audio experience.

🧭 How is it used?

The user positions their device camera. NetraSense continuously analyzes the walking path, providing directional acoustic sonar beeps and natural voice feedback. In emergencies, automated collision logging alerts trusted caregivers via Supabase.

📄 Detailed Page-by-Page Breakdown

1. Landing & Welcome Portal (/)

The gateway welcoming new navigators, caregivers, and doctors. Features the high-contrast brand mark logo, instant theme switcher (Sun/Moon), core feature pillars (Proximity Radar, Medical ID, One-Tap SOS), and direct access links into the dashboard.

2. Authentication & Access Hub (/auth)

Accessible sign-in and account registration powered by Supabase Auth. Also provides an instant "Continue as Guest / Demo Mode" button allowing evaluators and clinicians to immediately experience all features without an account.

3. Real-Time Assistive Command Center (/dashboard)

The heart of NetraSense. Displays the top gamified motivation cards (Obstacles Dodged Today, Safe Distance Explored, Active Assistance Time), the Blind's Eye AI Vision Lens with spatial zone analysis (Left, Center, Right), the Assistive Copilot Suite (Object Seeker, Face & Mood, Indoor Nav, Scene Reader), the Live Environment Radar with radial distance gauge (0 to 400 cm), and the Caregiver Quick-Glance Panel.

4. Emergency Contacts & Caregiver Hub (/contacts)

Manages primary and secondary caregiver profiles. Users can add trusted contacts, configure collision alerts, trigger instant phone calls with one tap (via tel: protocol), and run simulated emergency alert tests.

5. Emergency Medical ID Card (/medical-id)

A standardized medical card for paramedics, first responders, and good Samaritans. Highlights blood group, age, level of visual impairment (Partial, Legal Blindness, Total Blindness), critical medical allergies/notes, and primary emergency phone numbers. Includes a print-optimized layout for physical wallet cards.

6. Incident & Vision Telemetry Logs (/logs)

A searchable, filterable historical record of every obstacle detection and collision warning. Users and caregivers can filter by threat severity (Normal, Warning, Alarming / Collision) and export the entire dataset as a standardized CSV file.

7. Device & User Profile (/profile)

Manages patient information, impairment classification, emergency instructions, and residential address with instantaneous Supabase cloud sync.

8. Settings & Audio Preferences (/settings)

Comprehensive controls for tuning the speech synthesis engine (voice alerts on/off, announce normal readings, distance threshold slider), appearance mode, YOLO server endpoint URL, and external camera stream binding.

🔬 Dual-Tier Biometric Face Recognition Architecture

NetraSense features a resilient hybrid face identification engine that balances instant offline recognition with rich empathetic understanding:

⚡ Tier 1: In-Browser 512D Biometrics

Runs locally inside browserFaceMatcher.ts. Divides a normalized $128\times128$ face crop into an $8\times8$ cell grid with 8 gradient orientation bins. Normalizes lighting with CLAHE and matches via Cosine Distance in <50ms with 0 network latency, 0 token cost, and 0 rate limits.

💖 Tier 2: Multimodal Empathy & Mood

Uses a resilient fallback chain (gemini-2.5-flash $\to$ gemini-2.0-flash $\to$ gemini-1.5-flash) to detect facial expressions, smiles, fatigue, or distress, speaking warm prompts such as: "Your friend Sarah is smiling warmly."

🛡️ Cooldown & Fallback Safety

Includes built-in 3-second pacing and an automatic 10-second cooldown recovery mechanism to prevent API exhaustion while ensuring uninterrupted local biometric recognition.
